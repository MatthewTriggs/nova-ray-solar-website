About Nova Ray Solar

Local, hands-on solar installation for Gauteng homes and businesses.

Who We Are

Solar systems built to actually keep your lights on

Nova Ray Solar is based in Moreleta Park, Gauteng, and installs complete solar solutions, inverter, panels and battery storage, for households and small businesses across the area. We handle the full job: sizing the system to your usage, mounting the panels, wiring the inverter and battery, and signing it all off with a Certificate of Compliance.

Our team works with proven equipment from brands like Deye, LuxPower, Dyness and Lemoen, and every installation is backed by our own workmanship guarantee.

  • Free site inspectionsEvery quote starts with an on-site assessment of your roof, distribution board and household usage.
  • Certified installationsFull professional installation with COC included on every package, no hidden extras.
  • Quality componentsDeye and LuxPower inverters paired with Dyness or Lemoen lithium battery storage.
  • Local and reachableBased in Moreleta Park, speak directly to the team about your installation.
